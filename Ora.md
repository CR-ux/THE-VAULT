### The Blessing Economy

*Blessings* are given by listeners to artists on their profile page, through the click of a button.
This opens an invocation dialogue box, where the listener may optionally write their chosen sentiment to the creator they are blessing, to let them know how the work has moved them. 
Blessing an artist affects where, and what proportion, of the listener's monetary contribution (if any) is distributed.

Receiving
--





While not perfect (as long as value is considered monetarily), Ora plans to implement the following revenue share to creators:


## BY DEFAULT:
<br>

| Category       | %   | Distribution                                                                                     |
| -------------- | --- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Community      | 95  | Equally distributed amongst *all* creators uploading work to Ora, regardless of listening stats. |
| Infrastructure | 5   | See 'Infrastructure Share' below.                                                                |

## ON OPT-IN:
<br>

| Category       | %   | Distribution                                                                                           |
| -------------- | --- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Listening      | 50  | Proportional based on work listened to                                                                 |
| Blessing       | 25  | Proportional based on work 'blessed' by listener                                                       |
| Survival       | 15  | Equally divided amongst artists opted into the survival share, those experiencing hardship of any kind |
| Infrastructure | 10  | See 'Infrastructure Share' below.                                                                      |





### Infrastructure share
- For transparency, the 'infrastructure share' listed above refers to:
	- Covering server costs
	- development work
	- data backups
	- DNS
	- platform administration
	- community forum moderation
	- time spent on grant writing and getting the word out


### FYI 
- The percentages above apply to monetary donation/subscription.
- Mutual aid distribution is, by its nature, determined on a per-instance basis. 
	- Listeners may contact creators through the platform to organise information share/learning exchange, expertise, or any support that the creator may need. If said creator chooses, they may then make the work in question available to the listener.

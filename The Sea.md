<br>
<div style="font-size:12pt;">
<b>I.</b><br> <br>
the sea is singing <br>
saying in its breath:<br>
<br>
i am sorry i cant fix the bruise, the dose <br>
hold you in the weighty ways you wanted. <br>
i can't wash away any of the inside <br>
or make your mum call back earlier<br>
<br>
</div>



<div style="font-size:11pt;"><br>
<b>II.</b><br> <br>
the clouded sky had said then i am sorry <br>
that i had to let you go. <br>
i am sorry that the longing for your body <br>
can't make your work be held by those who need it <br>
<br>
yet,<br>
i asked them, could they tell my mum <br>
i am sorry i moved so far away  <br>
whatever water falls to all the gravity i ignored  <br>
was my children, they are singing.<br>
</div>



<br>
<div style="font-size:10.5pt;">
<b>III.</b><br> <br>
an egg cracked, and wondered <br>
why nothing had come out of it<br>
but i was there <br>
still, listening <br>
to its breaking <br>
as a wave.<br>
<br>
in blood <br>
in vain,<br>
and undried paint of endings<br>
that the egg made for the yolk <br>
it once wanted to<br>
or thought it could<br>
become.<br>
<br>
and the sand <br>
it landed on <br>
replied:<br>
i wish that i could help you<br>
feel safe in all your smallness, <br>
sighing.<br>

</div>
<br>


<div style="font-size:10pt;">
<b>IV.</b><br> <br>
how the grain <br>
it was the gospel to a<br>
god that (now, i know) just <br>
had to let me go.<br>
<br>
and i, the song that sang<br>
at bridges <br>
singing--<br>
to the beat of change.<br>
<br>
a weighted falling <br>
softly<br>
between concrete steps <br>
and into <br>
crumpled hats.<br>
</div>


<br>

<div style="font-size:9.5pt;">
<b>V.</b><br> <br>
i was the music with a rhythm, beaten <br>
dancing--<br>
round the fire, leaving.<br>
<br>
soot and ash in shapes of dancing <br>
bones for dancing, beaten skin <br>
for <br>
drumming.<br>
<br>
the pyre was as the wind then, but the leaves <br>
they were singing, listen <br>
to the ways they had forgiven <br>
all the reaching trees<br>
who'd had to let them go.<br>
<br>
for how <br>
else might they <br>
have grown?<br>
<br>
</div>



<div style="font-size:9.25pt;">
<b>VI.</b><br> <br>
i sighed, to someone then, i bellowed <br>
wretched as an organ<br>
or a vital body who <br>
when dreaming <br>
under blankets <br>
might have held<br>
the breath of this <br>
too late.<br>
<br>
a night of space <br>
between the twig<br>
of root<br>
of sickly and so brittle branches <br>
peeled,<br>
exposed of all that little filth <br>
it came from and became.<br>
<br>
and where it might sit at my keys <br>
a heavy oak's goodbye<br>
<br>
carved in two the unlocked seed<br>
unpalatable memory-- the setting <br>
of an undressed family <br>
table.<br>
<br>
</div>



<div style="font-size:9pt;"><br>
<b>VII.</b><br> <br>
i am forever in your art, <br>
and will<br>
to live <br>
as long as any fire<br>
that burns your heart <br>
to make it.<br>
<br>
this body is the work of living <br>
but death, it taught me everything<br>
<br>
there was no body made <br>
to hold the weld<br>
together.<br>
<br>
</div>



<br>
<div style="font-size:8.75pt;">
<b>VIII.</b><br> <br>
stitching all the eaves of mine<br>
together, mine the evening ore.<br>
<br>
to drowse from rousing, or how all <br>
the awe of all this<br>
storytelling <br>
left, arose <br>
from beds <br>
of rivers drenched and<br>
unforgiving.<br>
<br>
i was the children.<br>
<br>
i could never bear to hold now <br>
old.<br>
<br>
i do not want to die <br>
until some body holds <br>
the chest my ribs are washing onto shores <br>
<br>
writhed and underworked <br>
in salt<br>
a covering - <br>
<br>
</div>



<div style="font-size:8.5pt;">
<b>IX.</b><br> <br>
through it all i was <br>
the stitching <br>
<br>
of a signature <br>
or worse, the sign to which <br>
it gestured:<br>
<br>
a punched up joke, <br>
a line of fiction<br>
the unread letters to <br>
the character of you<br>
i wrote<br>
<br>
my selfhood out of <br>
just to hold, in holding <br>
or in trying to behold<br>
retrace the darkened spaces <br>
that your untied laces kicked me into <br>
<br>
</div>



<div style="font-size:8.25pt;"><br>
<b>X.</b> <br> <br>
emptied fathoms where i'm headed, <br>
there, where endless silence <br>
lights up, stages<br>
<br>
all these <br>
definitions <br>
<br>
of one <br>
<br>
word <br>
<br>
for how<br>
they could not <br>
<br>
ever hold <br>
<br>
my pages.<br>
<br>
</div>

<br>

<div style="font-size:8pt;">
<b>XI.</b> <br><br><br>
when out of frame <br>
there was <br>
a voice then, <br>
finally.<br>
<br>
it spoke for, <br>
of, and from all<br>
ages<br>
<br>
i was the children -- i was dancing<br>
i was the music -- was the dancing <br>
i was the song -- i bled from dancing <br>
<br>
was the book i wrote myself from -- <br>
tired<br>
<br>
still <br>
<br>
dancing<br>
<br>
</div>


<br>

<div style="font-size:7.5pt;">
<b>XII.</b> <br><br>
out of all <br>
the time in which<br>
<br>
i tied myself into <br>
the hope of what<br>
i was.<br>
<br>
i was inside this <br>
little story -- <br>
<br>
</div>


<br>
<div style="font-size:7.25pt;">
<b>XIII.</b> <br> <br>
right there, <br>
where i left myself <br>
<br>
to let it leave me<br>
<br>
writing<br>
nothing <br>
but a story. <br>
<br>
</div>

<br>

<div style="font-size:7pt;">
<b>XIV.</b><br> <br>
that read to me, in whispers, <br>
in the wind about my window<br>
<br>
once upon a life ago,<br>
one late and longing bedtime<br>
<br>
</div>

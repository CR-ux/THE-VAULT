  <h1>The Sea — a lexicomythographic braid</h1>
  <div class="credit emph">metacommentary interleaved with the poem; typography recedes like tide  [oai_citation:1‡The Sea.pdf](file-service://file-NKLoffKcRKn63Df9nAYyTt)</div>

  <!-- I -->
  <div class="stanza fade1">
    <span class="meta emph">Language arrives as surf: not to conquer, but to confess.</span>
    <span class="poem sea">the sea is singing</span>
    <span class="poem">saying in its breath:</span>
    <span class="poem">i am sorry i cant fix the bruise,</span>
    <span class="meta emph">Grief’s first law: the element apologizes, but does not repair.</span>
    <span class="poem">the dose</span>
    <span class="poem">hold you in the weighty ways you</span>
    <span class="poem">wanted.</span>
    <span class="poem">i can't wash away any of the</span>
    <span class="poem">inside</span>
    <span class="poem">or make your mum call back</span>
    <span class="poem">earlier</span>
    <span class="meta emph">The tide knows the phone won’t ring sooner; it can only keep time.</span>
  </div>

  <!-- III -->
  <div class="stanza fade2">
    <span class="meta emph">Myth shrinks to kitchen scale: breakfast becomes an oracle.</span>
    <span class="poem">an egg cracked, and wondered</span>
    <span class="poem">why nothing had come out of it</span>
    <span class="meta emph">Potential, all yolk, no hatching.</span>
    <span class="poem">but i was there</span>
    <span class="poem">still, listening</span>
    <span class="poem">to its breaking</span>
    <span class="poem">as a wave.</span>
    <span class="meta emph">The sea is a sound before it is a sight.</span>
  </div>

  <!-- V -->
  <div class="stanza fade3">
    <span class="poem">i was the music with a rhythm, beaten</span>
    <span class="poem">dancing--</span>
    <span class="poem">round the fire, leaving.</span>
    <span class="meta emph">Ritual keeps time for what cannot be kept.</span>
    <span class="poem">the pyre was as the wind then, but the</span>
    <span class="poem">leaves</span>
    <span class="poem">they were singing, listen</span>
    <span class="poem">to the ways they had forgiven</span>
    <span class="poem">all the reaching trees</span>
    <span class="poem">who'd had to let them go.</span>
    <span class="meta emph">Release is a pedagogy the branches learn from gravity.</span>
  </div>

  <!-- VIII -->
  <div class="stanza fade4">
    <span class="poem">stitching all the eaves of mine</span>
    <span class="poem">together, mine the evening ore.</span>
    <span class="meta emph">Language as seam, as little metal in the mouth of dusk.</span>
    <span class="poem">i was the children.</span>
    <span class="meta emph">A pronoun opens like a gate; plurality enters.</span>
    <span class="poem">i do not want to die</span>
    <span class="poem">until some body holds</span>
    <span class="poem">the chest my ribs are washing onto shores</span>
    <span class="meta emph">The body tries to beach itself into meaning.</span>
  </div>

  <!-- IX -->
  <div class="stanza fade5">
    <span class="poem">through it all i was</span>
    <span class="poem">the stitching</span>
    <span class="meta emph">Signature or sign? The hand trembles between being and pointing.</span>
    <span class="poem">of a signature</span>
    <span class="poem">or worse, the sign to which</span>
    <span class="poem">it gestured:</span>
    <span class="meta emph">Myth is the arrow, not the target.</span>
  </div>

  <!-- X -->
  <div class="stanza fade6">
    <span class="poem">emptied fathoms where i'm headed,</span>
    <span class="poem">there, where endless silence</span>
    <span class="meta emph">Silence is the largest instrument in the orchestra.</span>
    <span class="poem">lights up, stages</span>
    <span class="poem">all these</span>
    <span class="poem">definitions</span>
    <span class="poem">of one</span>
    <span class="poem">word</span>
    <span class="poem">for how</span>
    <span class="poem">they could not</span>
    <span class="poem">ever hold</span>
    <span class="poem">my pages.</span>
    <span class="meta emph">Paper refuses to become shore.</span>
  </div>

  <!-- XI -->
  <div class="stanza fade7">
    <span class="poem">when out of frame</span>
    <span class="poem">there was</span>
    <span class="poem">a voice then,</span>
    <span class="poem">finally.</span>
    <span class="meta emph">The frame recedes so the voice can enter.</span>
    <span class="poem">i was the children -- i was dancing</span>
    <span class="poem">i was the music -- was the dancing</span>
    <span class="poem">i was the song -- i bled from dancing</span>
    <span class="meta emph">Identity conjugates itself in rhythm until grammar loosens.</span>
  </div>

  <!-- XII–XIV (condensed) -->
  <div class="stanza fade8">
    <span class="poem">i tied myself into</span>
    <span class="poem">the hope of what</span>
    <span class="poem">i was.</span>
    <span class="meta emph">Hope is a knot that looks like a bow from far away.</span>
    <span class="poem">writing</span>
    <span class="poem">nothing</span>
    <span class="poem">but a story.</span>
    <span class="meta emph">Every myth is a manual for how to fail beautifully.</span>
  </div>

  <!-- XV -->
  <div class="stanza fade9">
    <span class="poem">Never</span>
    <span class="poem">be afraid to cry</span>
    <span class="poem">the sea is grateful</span>
    <span class="poem">for the salt</span>
    <span class="meta emph">Salt answers salt; the world keeps our tears like small fossils of weather.</span>
  </div>

  <!-- The weave begins: poem + commentary words interlaced -->
  <div class="stanza fade10">
    <span class="meta emph">Now the two currents mingle until distinctions silt over.</span>
    <span class="poem foam">never be afraid</span>
    <span class="meta emph">to let the tide speak for you</span>
    <span class="poem foam">to cry the sea is grateful</span>
    <span class="meta emph">because grief translates body into element</span>
    <span class="poem foam">for the salt</span>
  </div>

The ninth[^9] letter of the alphabet.

The Roman numeral for one.


pronoun used by a speaker to refer to himself, herself, themselves: *accept me for what I am.* 

*I*llegible, i.e. *I* do not owe you legibility. I do not owe you anything, and Opacity is my right. 
noun (the I) Philosophy
(in metaphysics) the subject or object of self-consciousness; the ego.

Usage:
Why is it incorrect to say ‘between you and I’ (rather than ‘between you and me’)? Why is it also wrong to say ‘John and me in my bedroom’ (instead of ‘(my) John and I in my bedroom’)? Should you say ‘she's much better than me’ or ‘she's much better than I’? For a discussion of such questions, see what you did to me.

abbreviation (I.)
[[Island]](s) or Isle(s) (chiefly on maps).

Iodine. Of atomic number 53, a non-metallic element forming black crystals and a violet vapour. From French iode (from Greek iōdēs ‘violet-coloured’, from ion ‘violet’ + -eidēs ‘like’)

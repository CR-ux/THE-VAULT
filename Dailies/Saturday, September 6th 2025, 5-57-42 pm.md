A reading of all pentacles in the cardinal directions suggests either ideas are coming to fruition and grounded reality, or your body itself is becoming the Truth you have fought for so long to find. 
the knight of pentacles faces east, peering upon the king of swords, and the feminine lover peeks out from under him, the chain attached to her left wrist visible; while only the sword of the masculine lover can be seen, its blade half concealed under the king of Swords himself. 
The full corn moon approaches, and the knight of pentacles in the Water (west) position is signalling to look forward from the past, but to honour and cherish the tumultuous and back breaking journey that has led you here. The Lord of Wild and Fertile Land appearing at this moment, in the realm of Memory, is no coincidence. Wand raised in his right hand, as a stag head with angel wings flies above him, seeming to kiss his head in gratitude. I think of your dead friend. I think of our dead friend. I think of my dead friend. 

The King of Swords is in the central position, facing the Knight, but looking down at the female lover, who stares directly at you. He is atop his chariot, that prince of the chariots of the Winds, Five cherubs surrounding him, and reminds you of Ezekiel; the mural on the wall that faces you; in this room. This room. This room. This room has held so much. And it is time to let it go, it is time now, more than ever. The Five angels, or maybe demons, remind you of the promise you made with your soul to embody the five male souls you now hold in your heart as queen, as humble queen to the other chess pieces that guide you. 
You resist the seven of pentacles in Earth position; perhaps for the failures it reminds you of. But these are sacred too, as you look at the two wilting and thorned flowers that point towards both the knight of pentacles and the ten. There is plenty of Taurus in this card; your Venus placement, and it is literally and symbollically mirrored by the Five of Pentacles in the Air position, pointing downwards, back at it. The giant taurean horns, certainly not your soul's Ram Horns, but your Hearts Horn of the Ox; the desire for warmth, for safety, for wellness. Perhaps at war with the very core essence of the identity you have built for yourself. This is an important time for replenishing the countless hours and nights and years of suffering energy, of strife and turmoil, screaming whether with your throat or with your hand, as it creates works endlessly, building worlds of dreamt up hands, crying out for another's to touch. 
Mercury, in both the reversed five of pentacles in Air, and the upright ten in Fire position, suggests a communication of ideas, of will, of thought. This may not be with another; but with yourself. Perhaps with the child you held for the first time recently, telling her:

## LAST NIGHT'S DREAM

in the dream, it was almost christmas. I was further south than i've been in years, walking along a brick wall. 
I must have been closer to childhood than I am now, although now I bring this to mind, I doubt the truth of it.
My sister was being lambasted for something; some insufficiency, and being ridiculed for wearing a hat. 
There was a gargantuan shopping mall, again, akin to the one I visited in hunger, not pleading, but trying to politely ask my father to buy me something to eat, as he ignored me, where I woke from to write that I am so hungry with no money to eat and my father doesn't care. 
There were three homeless people in the dream, who had taken the food in my bags, which I'd left on the street for a moment-- I wonder if these three were the same gods to whom I'd offered bread in the doorway of the library, when I'd just got out of hospital. They seemed rightfully angry at me, for I seemed to think it important to tell them of my own financial worries, of my own potential impending homelessness-- how selfish. 
I was trying to find, desperately, some sort of gift to replace either my loss or my lack of trying; so give some arbitrary gesture of care to the people who never really cared for the me I am, or tried their best to, but never enough. 
I don't know how much of the pain I've ever felt is real, or what real pain truly is, or whether pain is ever enough to be called real. 
I don't know how much of the family I've ever felt is real, or what family truly is, or whether family is ever enough to call me real.
I don't know how much of me I've ever felt at all, or what I truly am, or whether I am ever enough to be called real.

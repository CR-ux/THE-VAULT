## LAST NIGHT'S DREAM

in the dream, my father poured boiling water over my face. i felt the excruciating burns all over my skin
i showed my sister and told her to call the police 
I was playing a set in an outdoor festival, but from the sound desk; invisible 
The audio feed was cut prematurely, due to nobody enjoying the sounds 
I sat down at a focus group, one of many at the festival, which seemed to be hosted by my year 5 teacher, Mrs Deakin; who had once called me her favourite pupil 
It wasn't her 
Instead the others in the group started to mock and insult the composition I had tried to play during my set
I got up from the chair upset, and left the festival
I was suddenly by the roadside, near a petrol garage
I'd received a text from Dani, my abusive ex, and my first real love, saying: "I'm leaving for bulgaria tomorrow; if you even care anymore" -- I haven't seen him in maybe 6 years now; 
God, I've even seen him more recently than my father. 
I met him one last time. We held hands, for a second.
I remember that year we lived, miserable, in that flat, if you could even call it that; just two rooms. Were he'd have a seizure while I was inside him, from all the drugs we used to take, where I thought he was going to die, where I wanted to, where I wondered if it would be him who killed me on purpose, or me who killed him accidentally; or whether we would both just cease to exist physically like we had mentally.
Where we were holding each other, so many nights, so many of them crying, crying into one another's skin, forgetting whose tears were who's
Then, once he had left; my friend, Katie Blowers, from school who I've rarely thought about since, helped me move back to coventry 
It seemed my father had felt guilty about burning my face with the boiling water 
he had revealed a bedsit that he had owned for years, without telling me, letting me suffer in penury, starvation, poor health
It was a single room, off the ringroad in coventry 
I tried to get inside by the entrance was blocked by a small shoe-cabinet that one had to climb through, fashioned from the toy piano I recently performed on  
So humble, and yet, I loved it; I said 'if anyone only needs a single room, it's me' -- my father agreed, and left.
Katie was still there, helping me to settle in 
Suggesting I use the place for a little while, to get back on my feet 
I broke down, screaming how I couldn't do this again, I couldn't move again; I was stuck back here in Coventry for the rest of my miserable life
I had no energy left to fight back anymore 
Katie was trying to convince me that I couldn't stay in this awful place for long
I was tellilng her how at least I could make my murals here 

I woke up with the pain of the burns still on my face, 
Bern, my father's name 
I don't know why, but it feels like he will die soon.
He will die without me seeing him again, 
never having met the woman I am
in a single room

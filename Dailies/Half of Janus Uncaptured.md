## LAST NIGHT'S DREAM

in the dream, i was at a party
And there he was, again; that first fist to cross my skin 
I remember in my waking being bowled over on a beach, 
not in sleep, but in this waking dream--
I remember back then, and last night, playing some sort of useless game 

And in a different time, perhaps another place, I found a stitch of memory; 
I was placed as a thread inside a flimsy knit 
And held up by an ex lover, awkwardly, bent in half, and we were suspended in motion--
This told me everything then, but now, I struggle to find the reason
For I am, unreasonably, I am reason, unravelling itself. 

And in perhaps a different time, certainly another place, I was in an orchestra 
A group of instrumentalists who figure skated as they played 
And she was there again, my first kiss--
I know knot why the other scared and lost angels sent down to my primary school, as devils 
appear so frequently in dreams of late; too late, perhaps, perhaps of this I am certain 
But I am, certainly I am certainty unsure of itself.

We had hired gilla band, and two other groups, to headline the show, to accompany the skating portion of our performance 
And I remember wishing I'd had more input to the programming, as the best band was put on first; 
And this may have something to say about support, or the lack thereof, or the misplacing of it, 
But now all I recall, or perhaps mostly, or in part, is this party, 
And how it bled into this concert, in concert with my falling over as I spun in arms of my first kiss, at age 5--
Another time, then, it was another time then, I am sure;
Surely, perhaps it was time for me to fall over 
And the pain was undoubtable, still is; what unfathomable mystery is that of pain?
And I am, painfully I am pain numbed to itself.

Toward the end of this cycle, the photographer asked us to gather on the other side of an emptied moat; to take our group photograph 
And perhaps on purpose, definitely, I, seeking definition, never finding it, I placed myself in the centre of the line of musician-skaters
And bordering each side of this great looming trench there were doorways, a series of more than six, no more than twelve doorways, shuttered, opened, their tall, safe shutters of oak were opened in ostentatiousness 
And groups of three or four of us were gathered inside each doorway, while the photographer tried to find a position that captured us all 
At first the lens was visible to the three of us in the central opening I found myself as half of Janus within

And then he stepped left, and left again, not left but to his right; 
And the girls I sat in the midst of let out sounds of disappointment, while we leant to our right to try to see the lens, be witnessed, have our witnessing be witnessed--
And I tried to shout across the moat: you need to step back, you need more distance to fit us all in
But the distance was already too great, too great for my voice to travel down the grassy slope and back up again to find his ears; 
And there I was, 
I am, unwitnessed, I am witnessing, ignoring itself.

## LAST NIGHT'S DREAM

in the dream, i was a winner
i had won two million 
i had been announced the winner of a ridiculous competition 
about aesthetics and the way we look to one another 
such a trivial circumstance and yet i could barely believe my luck 
i was a model, then
not of plastic but of flesh,
and an elevator twisted our bodies into shapes i never thought possible. 
in the dream i had won a way out of all of this 
and i could not believe it 
and from this dream i awoke, into another dream, 
a realer one 
where, in a car in london 
i was waiting to be picked up by a group of people i thought i called my friends 
whatever that meant in this real dream 
and we were being driven to a place on the outskirts 
to find crystal meth, 
stopping along the way, for us to piss and shit on the roadside. 

and then i woke up from this dream 
into whatever this one is

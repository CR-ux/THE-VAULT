## LAST NIGHT'S DREAM

in the dream, i had sex with three different people. one of them was my grandfather; although i've never known or remembered him since he died when i was 1. he had the appearance of an old, old astrologer, I slept with in Poland, during that horrible month of needing to de-transition for safety. I didn't really want to sleep with him. I just needed to feel needed in that needlessly inauthentic and remembered state. He had told me, and convinced me for the first time, that the planets were simply a macrocosmic reenactment of the circular movement of molecules in our own bodies, and the particles yet inside of them. 
in my sleep, i came three times.

i was supposed to be looking after my little stepbrother, in a facsimile of my mother's new house. him and his friend were having a sleepover, and they'd gone to bed. so i left the house to go sleep with my grandfather. 
in his car, is where i think it happened. 
i had to return to the house for something. i can't remember what; but i realised my stepbrother was awake again, and i'd been caught out. 
i was brushing off his accusations; he opened a video on his phone that clearly showed this strange man, apparently my grandfather, crossing the lawn to come and pick me up. 
i tried to deny the whole thing, but ended up angrily leaving the house, which had two glass doors, clearly seeing out into the night. 
I slammed the door and caught the rollerblind cord inside of it, cracking the edge of it slightly. my stepbrother shouted "No!" as this happened. I left anyway. I think my grandfather and I, saved in my phone as 'Grandad Pitt' had sex again. 

Some time later, I was sitting on top of a train, almost like a model train, the one that travels out toward Reyn, the space of my subconscious; but this time, it was travelling inward, to the land beside, or above it. the train was slow enough to comfortably sit on top of, with some effort to make sure one didn't fall off. 

I felt wonderful on that train; knowing this was one of my favourite ways to travel, with all its danger, with all its solitude. Nobody thought to jump on top of these medium-speed trains; but they were so beautiful to ride on top of -- watching the mild scenery and all its mediocrity go by at a leisurely pace. 

I would eventually jump off the train, but not before it slowly struggled through a cityscape; one I've visited a few times, filled with walls, wall after wall, but this time, some were slightly lower, and I could, this time, see over them. 

the train, now free of its tracks, chugged slowly up this hill, scattered with people visiting various shops and sightseeing. I was the only one, still, on that train. I was manoeuvring my sitting position to make sure I didn't fall off.

The train went past Rhys, one of my first lovers; one of the deepest sexual connections I'd ever had; and we immediately decided we needed to sleep together again. In the waking world, I tried to text him and reconnect, not even in a sexual way, but he sent back an affronted text, saying how he was happy with his current girlfriend, as if I'd betrayed his new relationship somehow. We don't talk anymore. 

But in the dream, we went to his family home, and had to wait for all these children to leave the place, before we could fuck. Finally, the time came and he pulled me onto his parents' bed to start the whole thing again. I suggested we perhaps go to his room, surely, that would be more respectful, more discreet? He agreed. 

I believe at some point in the dream I had sex with Paul, and I remember his penis being small, something that I've been ashamed about mattering to me, but I didn't mind, not in the dream. But, perhaps because we've never met in person, not yet, but this meeting is to come in three days, perhaps this is why I can't recall our encounter in the dream -- only what's left in my underwear as the reminder of his presence within it, within me. 

It was such a detailed, long, tripartite dream. So much of it has gone now, and a past self would have spent the whole day writing, writing as survival, desperately trying to pull these symbols out from the ether, to see what it could all mean; in prayer, in prayer that things might change somehow -- surely, surely there must be a message from the other side, surely I can talk to them, those who've gone, who've left me, who I've left behind -- I might have thought in my body; the machine of all that is not is comprised of the dead. 

something is happening again; something with my heart, my hormones, something that has happened before. i know love, the romantic kind, i have been so sorely longing for, is very near. i have finished the greatest work of my life, even though truly it never fully can be finished; but Venus is ready now, to give me the rest that my heart has been crying out for 



I wake to find that Takk... has been released, today, as a 20th anniversary remaster. 
I don’t even care how well known it is. It fucking deserves to be. And I have so many memories. So many, more than any other album, of being in that kitchen overlooking a humble little garden, as a tiny tiny kid listening with my mum while peeling potatoes before I’d ever even seen her cry like she would every night towards the end, in that little kitchen looking at that garden where I’d play scrabble with her and my sister and sometimes my dad if he wasn’t too drunk, when I’d always win and they’d all get annoyed in a nice way, feeling like nothing was wrong; because i hadn’t seen how very, very wrong it all was yet, i was still in the Cave and watching the shadows of what i thought was my family, dancing, dancing— and the patio heater made this beautiful, quiet roar as it turned on, ready to warm our bones long into the dark, the smell of lavender and the feeling of bloated bumble bees brushing my bare ankles as they struggled to get back to their hive to wherever it was their own family had come from, none of it seemed wrong yet, it was the prologue, and this was the score

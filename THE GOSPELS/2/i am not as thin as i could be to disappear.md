Would you not throw yourself down and gnash your teeth and curse the demon who spoke thus? Or have you once experienced a tremendous moment when you would have answered him: "You are a god and never have I heard anything more divine."*
> 	-NoetNietzsche, The Gay Science.

When each permutation of events, text, experience has been obtained, the cycle begins again. Thus, Will is not free, at least not in the way this character, or perhaps you, understand it. Instead, the 'freeness' of our Will is an illusion of depth -- the sheer volume of choices available to us, though limited, are impossible to experience comprehensively. And this precludes our ability to see their finite nature.**

i remember this song
overlooking a humble little garden, as a small child, listening to this song, with my mum while peeling potatoes before I’d ever even seen her cry like she would every night towards the end, in that little kitchen, looking at that garden, where i’d play scrabble with her and my sister, and sometimes my dad if he wasn’t too drunk, when i’d always win and they’d all get annoyed in such an endearing way, when i felt like nothing was wrong; because i hadn’t seen how very, very wrong it all was yet, i was still in the Cave. and i was watching the shadows of what i thought was my family, dancing, dancing— and the patio heater made this beautiful, quiet roar as it turned on, ready to warm our bones long into the dark, the smell of lavender and the feeling of bloated bumble bees brushing my bare ankles as they struggled to get back to their hive, to wherever it was their own family had come from, none of it seemed wrong yet, it was the prologue, and this was the score

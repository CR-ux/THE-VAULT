

# Time
<br>

> *"In my [[notBorges/CRUCIBLE/Time|Beginning]] is my End." Four Quartets, T. S. Eliot*

<br>

In around 0.5 seconds, before[^b4] you have time[^H] to read these words, I will realise I am a [[Character.|character]] in a story. 
The person, if they may be called that, who is writing this in second person, is yet unaware that what they are doing is writing a story, by simply experiencing what they perceive as the 'current moment'. 
The word 'current'[^c] as it pertains to electricity, denotes the backward flow of negative charge, opposing the direction of electron movement from anode, the negative terminal, to cathode, the positive.
We believe the current moment to be something we experience from start to finish. But, just as Franklin imagined electricity positive, flowing forward, our understanding is in a state of reversal. 
We've got 'it', whatever the 'it' may be that we believe we have, backwards. 
This moment is in fact experiencing us; we create time with every sensory input, and with every definition of experience, we draw from a series of infinitely divisible memories to form a picture of what we believe to occur.[^oc]
